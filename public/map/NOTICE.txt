AtlasShare map geometry — data notices
======================================

@svg-maps/world 2.0.0
---------------------
Source: https://github.com/VictorCazanave/svg-maps
Package: https://www.npmjs.com/package/@svg-maps/world
License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)
         https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

AtlasShare modifications: removed the non-ISO `go` and `ju` paths; merged the
six `um-*` U.S. Minor Outlying Islands subdivisions into one `UM` path;
normalized display names; sorted units by ISO alpha-2 code. Palestine (`PS`)
and Israel (`IL`) remain separate paths.

Antarctica outline
------------------
Source: Natural Earth 1:10m cultural/physical data
        https://www.naturalearthdata.com/
License: public domain
        https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/

AtlasShare modifications: extracted a single main-ring Antarctica outline,
projected to the map's coordinate space, and committed it as
`scripts/data/antarctica-path.txt`. It is an approximate visual outline, not a
survey boundary.

The generated files `public/map/countries.json` and
`src/data/countries.generated.ts` are derived from the sources above. They
contain only public country/territory geometry and names; no user visit data is
included.
