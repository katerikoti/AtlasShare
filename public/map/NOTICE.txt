AtlasShare modifications: filtered the 256 source locations to the 195
recognized countries (193 UN member states plus the Holy See and Palestine);
removed all territories, Kosovo (`XK`), the non-ISO `go` and `ju` paths, and
the six `um-*` subdivisions; normalized display names; sorted countries by ISO
alpha-2 code. Palestine (`PS`) and Israel (`IL`) remain separate paths.

The generated files `public/map/countries.json` and
`src/data/countries.generated.ts` are derived from the source above. They
contain only public country geometry and names; no user visit data is included.
